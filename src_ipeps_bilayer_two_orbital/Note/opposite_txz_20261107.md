# Opposite-direction cross-orbital hopping, 2026-11-07

The collaborator confirmed opposite signs for x- and y-directed cross-orbital
hopping. The corrected model uses `TxzYSign=-1`: the tabulated `Txz1,Txz2`
are amplitudes, and the Hamiltonian uses `-Txz` horizontally and `+Txz`
vertically, for both cross-orbital channels and both layers. The checkerboard
type-1/type-2 mapping and all other terms are unchanged. The earlier
same-sign results are a separate [archive](cent_followup_same_sign_20261105.md).

The relative sign is a physical change. In an independent one-electron
plaquette check, `Tr(H^4)` changes from 1.70580704 to 0.77882976; a common
flip of both signs satisfies the orbital gauge equivalence. The full
Fock/Jordan-Wigner, graded Hamiltonian and analytic gate checks pass in both
symmetry builds, with maximum errors 4.44e-16 and 8.88e-16. The SU gates,
energy estimators, component estimators and format-2 cache all use the four
slots H1,H2,V1,V2. The corrected N-only smoke run matches its four-gate
cache replay; that cache rejects the other sign. The legacy same-sign smoke
energy is reproduced, and the new component sum closes to 8.88e-16/Ni.

At these parameters `(pi,0)` and `(0,pi)` are symmetry-related. The map
`(row,col) -> (col+1,row)` interchanges directions and flips the checkerboard
parity, preserving the type of every bond. An overall z-orbital phase flip
restores the directional hopping signs. We therefore compare with the
lower measured axial branch; a residual difference between two sampled
axial starts is not evidence of a physical orientation preference.

The Fourier convention is x=column and y=row. In these coordinates the
type-1 zigzag runs along the translation `(Delta x, Delta y) = (1,1)`
over two bonds. The minus diagonal has zero phase advance under that
translation, while the plus diagonal advances by pi. This distinguishes
the two measured orders independently of the choice of y-axis sign.
In the lowest measured D3 minus-diagonal state, the local moments in each
layer point oppositely across all 16 type-1 bonds and in the same direction
across all 16 type-2 bonds. The lowest plus-diagonal and axial states each
have 8 opposite and 8 parallel pairs of local moments per bond type and
layer. This is a check of the measured one-site moment pattern, not a
measurement of two-site spin correlations.

## Artifacts and scope

The four D4 cases (array 168701) and all nine adjusted-exchange cases (array
168702) converged at Chi32 without a retry, and retained their initial
wavevectors in both layers. The D4 jobs took 38.2–41.0 minutes each on one
physical core; peak RSS was 1.445 GiB.
Per-case inputs, final wavevectors, energies and checkpoint fingerprints
are summarized in `opposite_txz_20261107.csv`; file hashes and grouped
energies are in `opposite_txz_20261107_provenance.json`. Full collected JSON,
manifests and figures are in local ignored `run/ipeps_opposite_txz_20261107/`.
The full states and logs remain in the isolated cent deployment. Recreate
the sign/D comparison with `plot_opposite_txz.py` and the component figure
with `plot_energy_components.py` from the collected inputs. The constrained
exchange figure uses `plot_followup.py --scan` with the D3 and exchange collections.

These are finite-D simple-update comparisons of sampled collinear starts.
CTMRG convergence for fixed states does not establish convergence in
projection time or over the variational space. With both interlayer terms
zero the Hamiltonian separates into independent layers in each fixed
layer-charge sector, but the rung ansatz shares D across the layers.
A larger-D single-layer run at the measured balanced filling can help
separate this resource constraint from the physical ordering.
