# cent N-only D=2,3,4 initial screen, 2026-11-05

> Historical same-sign model (`TxzYSign=+1`, including omitted legacy keys).
> On 2026-11-07 the collaborator confirmed opposite x/y signs. The energies
> and candidates here must not be interpreted as results for that corrected model.

At the original table parameters, both sampled diagonal branches move **higher
relative to the (0,pi) branch** as D increases in this matched, short-time
screen. The midpoint shows the same direction of change. This does not support
expanding a diagonal-favored parameter region toward the table on the basis of
larger D alone. It is an observation at fixed projection time and Chi, not a
converged statement about the phase diagram.

energy units. Negative means that diagonal branch is lower than this

## Validation and interpretation limits

All 32 cases completed; status counts: `{'complete': 32}`. Every final
CTMRG converged, and every actual bond dimension equals its requested D.
The maximum filling deviation is 2.54e-07/Ni;
maximum local |Sx| is 9.74e-14. Each completed process
has one-CPU affinity and numerical thread settings equal to one.
D4 cases took 17.1–18.8 minutes on one core; the maximum
observed child RSS across all cases was 0.703 GiB.


The aligned-layer FM control has measured total Sz=1.5/rung. The target's
exact-sector metadata has Sz=null. The physical rung index has five N sectors
rather than the 15 N,Sz sectors of the two-U1 target; physical and virtual
indices both carry only N. Independent validator checks also cover
transverse-spin coherences. The Hamiltonian's
intrinsic Sz blocks accelerate gate construction, then every coefficient is
lifted into the N-only gate; they do not impose spin symmetry on the PEPS.

Nevertheless, the principal starts are collinear definite-Sz product states,
and their evolved transverse moments remain near numerical zero. Removing
spin U(1) does not automatically search every magnetization sector, canted
state or spiral. These data compare sampled collinear branches. The aligned
FM control tests one nonzero-Sz start, not exhaustive spin-sector exploration.

Other important limits are the short projection time, tau=0.03 final step,
one chosen relative spin phase and charge orientation per main branch, and
D-dependent contraction errors at fixed Chi=8. CTMRG convergence at one Chi
does not bound that latter error. Both layers share the rung D, and the zero
interlayer couplings leave their relative spin orientation energetically
undetermined. The reference is specifically (0,pi), not the best competitor
at every point/D; (pi,0) was checked only at candidate D3 in this campaign.

A matched longer simple-update continuation and smaller final tau are needed
before interpreting this as a converged D trend. A promising result would
then warrant the Chi checks already deferred by the user. The earlier local
two-U1 continuation notes used different projection histories and sometimes
the (pi,0) reference; their numbers must not be mixed into this table.
